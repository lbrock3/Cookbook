FAMILY COOKBOOK: HOW TO USE THE RECIPE EDITOR
=============================================

The editor lets you add, change and delete recipes right on the website,
with no code and no re-uploading. Only the keeper (you) can use it.
Visitors never see the editor.


1. SIGN IN
----------
  1. Open your cookbook (cookv2.com).
  2. Scroll to the very bottom of any page.
  3. Click "Keeper sign-in", type your keeper password, click Sign in.
  4. The footer now says "Keeper mode is on", and an "Edit Recipes" link
     appears in the menu bar at the top.

  Click "Sign out" in the footer when you are done, especially on a
  shared or public computer. If you wait a long time, the sign-in ends on
  its own and you just sign in again.

  Your password lives in the file config.php on your GoDaddy hosting
  (cPanel > File Manager > public_html). To change it, edit that file there.
  If keeper sign-in is missing from the footer, config.php still has the
  default password CHANGE-THIS-PASSWORD.


2. ADD A NEW RECIPE
-------------------
  1. Sign in, then click "Edit Recipes" in the menu, then "Add a recipe".
     (Shortcut: on any chapter page, click "Add a recipe to this chapter".)
  2. Pick the Chapter the recipe belongs in.
  3. Fill in the form (details in section 4).
  4. Click Preview to see exactly how it will look.
  5. Click Save recipe. It appears on the site right away, in its chapter
     and in search.


3. EDIT AN EXISTING RECIPE
--------------------------
  Option A: open the recipe, then click "Edit this recipe".
  Option B: Edit Recipes in the menu, find it in the list, click Edit.

  Change anything, click Preview if you like, then Save recipe.

  To MOVE a recipe to a different chapter, change the Chapter box at the top
  of the form and click Save recipe. Its comments and photo go with it. Old
  links, menu entries, and visitors' saved favorites and grocery lists keep
  working, because the site remembers where the recipe went. Edit Recipes
  lists it with a "Moved" tag. If you move it back later, it simply returns.


4. THE FORM, FIELD BY FIELD
---------------------------
  Recipe title       Required. Each title needs to be different within its
                     chapter. If you see "A recipe with that web address
                     already exists", change the title a little.
  Submitted by       Who sent the recipe in.
  Recipe from        Where it came from, like "Grandma Brock".
  Short description  One or two sentences about what makes it good.
  Prep / Cook /      Free text, like "15 minutes" or "1 hour".
  Total time
  Servings           Like "4" or "12 cookies".
  Difficulty         Easy, Moderate or Involved. Optional.
  Ingredients        ONE INGREDIENT PER LINE. Press Enter between them.
  Directions         ONE STEP PER LINE. Do not type step numbers; they are
                     added for you.
  Photo              Optional. Choose a picture of the finished dish. It is
                     shrunk for you, so any size works. Use "Remove photo"
                     to take it off. Click Preview to see it.
  Special tools      Separate with semicolons: Dutch oven; thermometer
  Notes              Tips, substitutions, or the story behind the recipe.

  Only the title is required, but a recipe with ingredients and directions
  is far more useful.

  Ingredient groups
    To put a heading inside the ingredient list, start the line with a
    label and a colon:

        Sauce: 2 tablespoons soy sauce
        1 teaspoon sugar
        Marinade: 1 pound flank steak

    Lines after a labeled line stay in that group until the next label.

  Writing ingredients so the Grocery List works well
    The Grocery List adds amounts together across recipes, so it helps to
    put the amount first, then the unit, then the item:

        2 cups flour            (good)
        1 1/2 pounds chicken    (good)
        1 can (15 oz) beans     (good)
        flour, 2 cups           (will not combine as well)

    Fractions like 1/2 and 1 1/2 are fine. "Salt to taste" is fine too;
    it just shows up with no amount.


4b. RECIPES SENT IN BY THE FAMILY
---------------------------------
  Anyone can send a recipe from "Share a Recipe". Nothing shows in the book
  until you approve it.

  1. When something is waiting, a number appears next to "Edit Recipes" in
     the menu, and the footer says how many are waiting.
  2. Open Edit Recipes. Under "Waiting for your approval", click Review.
  3. The recipe opens in the editor with everything filled in, and the
     sender's photo if they added one. Fix anything you like.
  4. Click Save recipe to add it to the book, or "Reject this one" (then
     "Yes, reject") to throw it away. A rejected recipe and its photo are
     deleted for good.


5. DELETE A RECIPE
------------------
  Open the recipe's Edit page and click "Delete this recipe", then "Yes, delete" (or "Keep it" to cancel).
  It is hidden from the site, but not lost forever (see section 6).


6. UNDO AND RESTORE
-------------------
  Edit Recipes lists everything you have changed, tagged:
    Added    a recipe you created
    Edited   an original recipe you changed
    Deleted  a recipe you removed
    Moved    a recipe you moved to another chapter (just a note; the
             recipe itself is listed under its new chapter)

  Undo my changes   puts an edited original recipe back exactly as it was.
  Restore           brings back a deleted recipe.

  Your own added recipes have no "original" to go back to; delete them if
  you do not want them.


6b. NOTES AND BACKUPS
---------------------
  "Made it?" notes: visitors add these under each recipe. As keeper you
  can delete any of them with the Delete link beside the note.

  Backups: the site saves a copy of the database by itself about once a
  day (the last 14 are kept). At the bottom of Edit Recipes, "Download a
  full backup" gives you a zip with the database and all photos. Keep one
  somewhere off the server now and then.


7. WHERE YOUR CHANGES ARE KEPT
------------------------------
  Edits are saved on the server in the "data" folder, together with the
  comments and discussion. Photos are in the "photos" folder. The original
  recipes are built into index.html.

  - Replacing index.html with a newer version does NOT erase your edits.
  - Back up the "data" and "photos" folders regularly. If they are lost,
    your edits, comments and photos are lost.
  - Never overwrite or delete "data", "photos" or config.php when updating
    the site.


8. TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------
  "Your keeper sign-in ended"       Sign in again at the bottom of the page.
  "That photo couldn't be used"     Try a different picture (JPEG or PNG).
  "Couldn't save"                   Check your internet or server, then try
                                    again. Your text is still in the form.
  "Please give the recipe a title"  Fill in the Recipe title box.
  No "Edit this recipe" button      You are not signed in as keeper.
  Changes not showing               Refresh the page (Ctrl+Shift+R on a PC,
                                    Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).
  Editor says it is part of the     You opened the preview copy. The editor
  GoDaddy version                   only works on the hosted site.

  Tip: write long recipes in a text editor first, then paste them into the
  Ingredients and Directions boxes. Each pasted line becomes one item.
